How cpus.csv uses this: rows with est=0 have index = 100 * sqrt( (Dhry2Opt/1595) * (MWIPS/816) ), the geometric
mean of integer and FP speed relative to the PIII 1000. The other rows use MHz/10 * a per-clock factor fitted to these
anchors and adjusted from memory of game benchmarks [EST]. For example, Athlon 64 and Core 2 rank higher in games than in
Whetstone because of memory latency and cache.
